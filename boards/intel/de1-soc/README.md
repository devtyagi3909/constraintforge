# DE1-SoC

**Device:** 5CSEMA5F31C6N (Cyclone V SoC) | **Vendor:** Terasic

**Covered:** 4× 50 MHz clocks, 10 LEDs, 6 seven-segment displays, 4 buttons, 10 switches, GPIO headers, HPS UART/DDR3/Ethernet/SD.

**Files:**
- `de1-soc.qsf` — pin assignments
- `de1-soc.sdc` — timing constraints (include both in your project)
